## About Me

I'm an M.S. student at **KAIST**, working on **AI for Science**.

With a background in **Biomedical Science & Engineering** and
**Computer Science & Engineering** at Konkuk University, I'm interested
in connecting advances in machine learning with scientific problems.

My research spans **multimodal debiasing, knowledge distillation,
preference alignment, and scientific agents**. I'm particularly
interested in how models can use evidence more reliably, and how
agents can turn past experience into better decisions.

Currently, I am exploring **LLM agents for protein design**:
organizing structural observations and design outcomes into reusable
skills that can guide an agent's initial design decisions.

## Publications & Preprints

### 2026

#### BUZZY: Contrastive Scoring to Mitigate Text-Induced Bias in Multimodal Multiple-Choice QA

Taeyun Roh, Suhyeong Park, Dongyoung Lee, Wonjune Jang,
Junha Jung, **EunYeong Jo**, Jaewoo Kang

**arXiv preprint · 2026**

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28026)
· [Project Page](https://txxnrd.github.io/buzzy/)
· [Code](https://github.com/dmis-lab/Buzzy)

A training-free contrastive scoring method that reduces text-induced
bias in multimodal multiple-choice question answering. BUZZY
subtracts text-only candidate preferences from multimodal scores
to emphasize evidence contributed by the image.

- Evaluated across **five VLM backbones and five benchmarks**.
- Improves average accuracy while reducing inference latency by
  **over 28%** compared with prior multimodal contrastive approaches.

#### Image-Guided Debiasing Distillation with Preference Alignment Across Multi-News Histories

Jimyeung Seo, **Eun-Yeong Jo**, Hye-Yoon Baek, Dongcheon Lee,
Xiongnan Jin, Byungkook Oh

**WSDM 2026 · Full Paper · pp. 574–584**

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3773966.3777983)

Research on image-guided debiasing distillation and preference
alignment for robust knowledge extraction across news histories.

- Introduces **Triple-level Supervised Fine-Tuning (TriSFT)**.
- Combines image-guided distillation with preference alignment
  to improve knowledge extraction.

## Current Research

### ProtSkill — Reusable Structural Experience for Protein Design Agents

**Research in progress**

Can an agent make a better first design by learning from previous
protein-design experience?

I am exploring a workflow that connects:

**Design & Evaluation → Structural Analysis → Experience Memory
→ Reusable Skills → Initial Design Planning**

The project focuses on:

- **Structural experience**
  Connecting target-site properties, binder structures, interface
  contacts, design settings, and evaluation outcomes.

- **Evidence-grounded skills**
  Recording actionable strategies with supporting cases,
  counterexamples, applicability, and limitations.

- **Executable design specifications**
  Connecting an agent's DesignSpec to validation, build, and
  concrete protein-design tool inputs.

- **Experience transfer**
  Investigating whether retrieved knowledge improves initial
  design decisions for new targets and reduces costly evaluation
  and redesign.
